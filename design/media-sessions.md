# How Playback Will Work in Snowgroove

snowgloo had the client device (usually a phone) drive all playback.

A single client could only ever play music on one target. Whether that be the phone or an external speaker.

snowgroove will push the ownership of playback sessions from the clients up to the snowgroove server.

This means a client will need to be able to see what devices currently have active playback sessions.

A client will also need to manage the queues of multiple devices.

It would be ideal to have music playing on your device, then be able to manipulate music for the kids on a speaker, without stopping the phone's audio or jacking up its queue.
